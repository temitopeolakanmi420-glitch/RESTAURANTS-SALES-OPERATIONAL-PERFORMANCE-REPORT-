## 🔍 Project Overview
Managing restaurant operations across multiple zones requires continuous tracking of order volume, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction[cite: 7]. This project analyzes order data to answer critical operational questions:
* *Which delivery zones generate the highest order volume and customer demand[cite: 7]?*
* *How do average delivery times fluctuate throughout different periods of the day[cite: 7]?*
* *Which cuisines and payment methods are most preferred by customers[cite: 7]?*

---

## 📊 Executive Summary & Key Metrics
* **Total Orders**: 299,000 Orders[cite: 7]
* **Total Quantity Sold**: 2,000 Units[cite: 7]
* **Average Delivery Time**: 30.54 Minutes[cite: 7]
* **Average Customer Rating**: 3.36 out of 5.0[cite: 7]

---

## 🚨 Business Problems Identified
1. **Moderate Customer Satisfaction (3.36 / 5.0)**: The average rating indicates room for improvement in food delivery quality, accuracy, or transit time[cite: 7].
2. **Evening Delivery Bottlenecks**: Average delivery times peak in the **evening (33 minutes)** compared to morning and afternoon windows[cite: 7].
3. **Heavy Cash Preference**: **66.2%** of orders are paid via Cash, compared to **33.8%** via Card, requiring efficient cash collection and courier management[cite: 7].
4. **Zonal Order Disparity**: **Zone D** leads significantly with 51,000 orders, while **Zone A** logs the lowest demand at 15,000 orders[cite: 7].

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

1. **Optimize Evening Delivery Operations**:
   * Reallocate delivery riders to peak evening hours to reduce transit delays from 33 minutes down to the 29–30 minute target[cite: 7].
2. **Targeted Service Improvements for Ratings**:
   * Address factors driving lower ratings (3.36/5.0) by establishing partner restaurant packaging standards and tracking food temperature at dispatch[cite: 7].
3. **Expand Marketing in Lower-Performing Zones**:
   * Deploy localized promotions in **Zone A (15K orders)** and **Zone C (22K orders)** to balance order distribution across all operational zones[cite: 7].
4. **Promote Digital Payment Options**:
   * Introduce digital wallet incentives to shift customers from Cash (66.2%) to Card/Online payments, reducing courier cash-handling risks[cite: 7].
